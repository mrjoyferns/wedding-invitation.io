Our Story

Some stories begin with a simple hello.

Ours began with two hearts, two journeys, and a love that slowly became something beautifully certain.

We learned that love isn’t measured by the distance between two people, but by the effort they make to stay close. Through different time zones, long conversations, missed hugs, goodbyes, countless calls, every “I love you,” every “I miss you,” and every countdown until we could meet again — all we wished for was to be together, and our love continued to grow.
